Cinematic Influences

Directors and visual traditions shaping my approach to framing and rhythm.

Visual Tension

Sergio Leone

Mastery of temporal expansion—stretching silence and environmental detail before sudden, decisive action. His interplay between extreme wide shots and tight eye-line close-ups heavily informs my blocking.

Blocking & Weather

Akira Kurosawa

Treating natural elements—rain, wind, smoke—as active emotional forces inside the frame, and using multi-plane staging to guide the viewer's eye without heavy exposition.

Precision & Tone

David Fincher

Disciplined camera movement motivated strictly by character psychology, paired with low-key lighting palettes and clinical editorial pacing.
